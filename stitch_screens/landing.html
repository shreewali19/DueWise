Root Cause Analysis

Why students struggle with deadlines

Field research across 400+ undergraduate engineering students identified 3 chronic operational failure points in coursework tracking.

CASE REF // 01 history_edu

Fragmented handwritten notes

Notes scribbled on notebook margins, loose sheets, or blackboard snapshots get buried under subsequent lecture pages. By revision week, assignment specifications are completely lost.

Vulnerability: Physical Loss • Zero Searchability
CASE REF // 02 forum

No centralized alert system

Cutoffs are broadcasted chaotically across unmoderated WhatsApp class groups, disparate Google Classrooms, and physical department notice boards without synchronized reminders.

Vulnerability: Information Blindspots • Late Discovery
CASE REF // 03 layers_clear

Multi-subject assignment chaos

Balancing 6 to 8 simultaneous theory subjects with intense lab report cutoffs leads to deadline collisions where low-weight tasks cannibalize high-credit project milestones.

Vulnerability: Cognitive Overload • Panic Pulling
Comparative Flaw Analysis

Why existing methods fail under semester stress

Paper Diaries STATIC

Completely passive. They never ping, buzz, or alert you while you are engrossed in another subject. One forgotten bag means zero visibility.

Outcome • Missed weekend deadlines
Manual Alarms CONTEXTLESS

A ring with no context is swiped away in seconds. Alarms lack links to lab rubrics, formatting guidelines, or file submission requirements.

Outcome • Dismissed and forgotten
Generic To-Do Apps UNSTRUCTURED

Commercial checklists intermix grocery runs with 20-page VLSI hardware write-ups. No concept of credit weights, courses, or college semesters.

Outcome • Noise and prioritization drift
Spreadsheets HIGH-FRICTION

Excel sheets require high maintenance on mobile. They lack push engines, automatic time calculations, and rapid one-tap completion logs.

Outcome • Abandoned after Week 3
